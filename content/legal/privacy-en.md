## 2. The two roles in which we process data

The distinction below is essential to understanding this policy.

**We are the CONTROLLER** of your account data: your name, email address, billing details, platform usage logs and your correspondence with us. We process this data in order to provide the service to you.

**We are a PROCESSOR** for the financial documents you upload. If you are an accountant or adviser uploading trial balances, financial statements or other documents belonging to your clients, you (or your client) remain the controller of that data. We process it solely on your instructions, for the purpose of producing the financial analysis you requested.

Using the platform for your clients' documents constitutes a data processing agreement between us, on the terms described in this policy and in the Terms of Service.

## 3. What data we process

**Account data:** first and last name, email address, preferred language and currency, account settings.

**Authentication data:** if you sign in with Google, we receive the email address and name associated with your Google account. We never receive or store your Google password.

**Uploaded financial documents:** trial balances, financial statements, sales files and other accounting documents you upload, together with the values extracted from them and the analyses generated.

**Billing data:** if you purchase a subscription, payment data is processed directly by Stripe. We do not store your card details on our servers.

**Technical data:** IP address, browser and device type, time of access, pages visited and errors encountered. We use this for security, diagnostics and improving the service.

**Correspondence** you send us by email or through in-app forms.

## 5. Artificial intelligence and model training

We consider this section essential and state it without ambiguity.

**Your financial data is not used to train artificial intelligence models** — neither by us nor by our providers.

The platform uses language models provided by Anthropic to explain, verify and comment on results. Numerical computation is performed by our deterministic engine, not by the language model. Where document content is transmitted to Anthropic for processing, it is transmitted through the commercial API, which does not use submitted data for model training.

For documents in languages or formats for which we do not have a deterministic parser, value extraction is assisted by the language model. Those results are visibly labelled in the interface.

## 7. How long we keep data

**Account data:** for the life of the account and 30 days after its deletion.

**Uploaded documents and generated analyses:** until you delete them or delete your account. We delete them permanently within 30 days of the request.

**Billing data:** 10 years, as required by Romanian tax and accounting law.

**Technical and security logs:** up to 12 months.

**Backups:** up to 35 days, after which they are overwritten in the normal rotation cycle.

## 8. Your rights

As a data subject you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and the right to withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

You can exercise these rights by writing to privacy@cfo-ai.io. We respond within 30 days at the latest.

You can delete your account directly in the application, under Settings. Deleting your account deletes the documents you uploaded and the associated analyses.

If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP)**, B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru 28-30, Sector 1, Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro.

## 9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, role-based access control, database-level isolation between accounts, access logging and automated security checks in our development process.

No system is absolutely secure. In the event of a personal data breach posing a risk to the rights and freedoms of data subjects, we notify ANSPDCP within 72 hours and, where the law requires it, inform the affected individuals.
