## P3 — FOCUS WORKS LIKE HOVER; ESCAPE DISMISSES

On a statement cell: hover opens the card; the pointer leaving closes it;
a **real Tab keystroke** from the previous figure lands on it
(`document.activeElement === el`) and the card opens with no delay; Escape
closes the card and leaves focus where it was.

Two things measured on the way, kept as comments in the spec:

- the pointer must leave **straight down, in steps**. The card opens to the
  LEFT of a statement cell, and a jump towards the top-left corner crosses
  Radix's grace area between trigger and content, which keeps the card
  open by design — `mouse.move(2,2)` left it open; 200 px straight down
  closed it. That is a fact about Radix, not a defect.
- `el.focus()` opens the card without delay on every one of the 47
  statement cards and every finding card; the Tab law exists because a
  synthetic focus event would pass with `tabIndex` deleted (the unit
  suite already recorded this).
