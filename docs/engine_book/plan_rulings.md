## 27. Rulings (final)

Markers: [UNCHANGED] as in scenarios_rulings.md; [CHANGED] with what changed and why; [NEW] not in scenarios_rulings.md. B0 copies this section to docs/engine_book/plan_rulings.md.

R1 BASE REVENUE GROWTH [CHANGED]. Never a silent 0. Ladder: [book] multi-period CAGR over eligible prior periods (1.4); else [macro] the ro_macro inflation anchor with source and stated_as_of (jurisdiction RO); else [convention] 0 with the terminal-rung sentence. Base growth is margin-neutral: variable costs grow with it, fixed costs with inflation (5.3). The blast radius is measured by scripts/measure_plan_blast_radius.py and handed to the owner before the Scenarios cut-over and the forecast flag flip. Changed: the third rung was [engine_default], a tier the vocabulary does not have, and the engine reads growth through micros(), which cannot take a null (blocker amendment); the margin-neutral clause was added because the plan/1 formula rebuilt defect 0.1 on the growth path.

R2 REVENUE LEVER [CHANGED]. volume_index and price_index are level shocks (level_pct, never compounding). A per-year growth change in the cockpit is an override of revenue_growth (compounds, labelled so); growth_pp is emitted only by templates and presets, never by a user control; any user-sourced shock on revenue_growth, whatever its op, is refused (growth_change_is_an_override), so a growth change has one request form. Cost of sales follows volume_index and input_price_index, never the selling price_index. Changed: R2 had the cockpit sliders send growth_pp, which gave one economic change two request forms and two lever_set_hash values.

R3 OTHER OPERATING INCOME [CHANGED]. Held fixed as other_operating_income_annual (money, tier book, user-overridable per year, sliced by days); it scales with neither volume, growth nor inflation. Changed: the served key is renamed from a share of revenue to an annual amount so its name matches its behaviour.

R4 RUNWAY [CHANGED]. runway {months, bound exact | at_least, first_shortfall_period, window_months, sentence, facility_limit refused "facility limit not loaded"}, measured on cash_before_funding = closing_cash - revolver against min_cash; a shortfall in an annual period is at_least with a sentence, never interpolated. The headline also shows first_shortfall_period, peak funding gap and funding interest at the book's own rate. Changed: "null when it never does" could not feed "holds for n months", the month of an annual-period shortfall was undefined, and the per-period engine value restarts from floored cash.

R5 DSCR [CHANGED]. formula_id dscr_ltm = LTM EBITDA / (LTM interest on debt and on the funding line + closing st_debt + closing revolver), part-actual while fewer than twelve projected months exist; breaches are predicates (EBITDA < t x denominator), never ratio comparisons; the formula equals the ratio table's dscr at the anchor on books without capitalised own work. Changed: the plan/1 reading applied an annual ratio to monthly flows (month-1 agras 0.7036 against an annual 7.4345) and ignored the revolver.

R6 ROUTE [CHANGED]. POST /api/forecast/{period_id}/recompute with a module-scope Pydantic model (CLAUDE.md 22). GET /api/forecast/{period_id}?horizon=3|5 equals, by body_hash, a POST of {horizon: {total_years: h, monthly_months: 12}} with want omitted. No path ending /scenario is mounted. The entry point is engine.forecast.levers.project_plan. Changed: names only (was /scenario and project_levers).

R7 HORIZON [CHANGED]. Two fields: monthly_months in {12, 24} and total_years in 1..5 (filled from levers.yaml#scenarios.total_years when omitted), invariant total_years x 12 >= monthly_months; horizon_years leaves KEYS; ALLOWED_HORIZONS stays years; monthly periods carry year_offset (k - 1) // 12 + 1; year_one_granularity is removed. Changed: the year_offset and granularity clauses were added.

R8 BASES [CHANGED]. DPO on trade payables (the ratio table's operand) over cost of sales, served as dpo_cogs_days, beside dio_cogs_days, both labelled days of cost of sales with the ratio table's own value and formula id shown beside them. Capex keeps the engine's derivation (depreciation / revenue), stamped tier convention with a rule id. Changed: the keys are renamed because the ratio table divides by total operating expense and the same names would show two values; the capex stamp is convention, not book.

R9 NET INCOME [UNCHANGED]. Statutory anchor (account 121) wherever an actual is served, including the base actual column (3.14); the reconstruction only as a labelled fallback.

R10 COVENANTS [CHANGED]. User covenants are the firm_covenants rows of the period's org (firm_id null or the org's organizations.firm_id), selected on org_id through the caller's client with the firm match applied in the engine, and a table that is not visible serves band tracks with first_breach_period refused covenants_not_migrated, never a 500; on money metrics and the forecast ratio ids of 9.1; tracks test every modelled month. Pack reference bands render as "reference band" with first_crossing_period, never "breach". Strip and verdict breach slots read user tracks only, else "no lender term is loaded". DEFAULT_RO_COVENANTS is deleted at the cut-over. Changed: R10 said no user covenant was loadable; the table already admits member rows with a null firm, so the branch is reachable and gated.

R11 KEY-ACCOUNT LOSS TEMPLATE [CHANGED]. Bound to the top counterparty's share of the trade-receivable balance, recomputed in integer minor units and micros from the leaves the concentration detector ranked (the detector, run in process from the period's own line items independent of the radar env flags, decides whether it fires; its float share is never bound), used as a stated proxy for revenue share; refused with the detector's reason when it does not fire; never 76 percent. Changed: the served radar finding is unmounted by default, so binding to it would make the template unreachable.

R12 MACRO PRESETS [CHANGED]. Only sourced series render: ro_macro inflation for RO periods, as presets at the anchor's own published band; they add only their step (the anchor is already in the default), move fixed-cost inflation and input prices, and move selling prices only through an explicit pass-through (initial 0, stated on the card). Rates and FX serve "no sourced series". Changed: the compile target and the no-stacking rule were added.

R13 CAPSULE WHAT-IF TOOL [CHANGED]. run_scenario_preview is routed through engine.forecast.levers.project_plan: revenue pct to a volume_index level shock, expenses pct to pool_level shocks on every served opex pool of the book, absolute mode refused with a pointer to the Scenarios page, values as decimal strings only. Changed: the entry-point name, and the mapping, which R13 left undefined.

R14 COST BEHAVIOUR [NEW]. Opex pools are split from statement_line_items by RO account prefix (pack data), with a named unallocated residual and a refusal above a pack share; cost_of_sales is fully variable and never fitted, carries a pool_fixed_share key (and no pool_level key), and changes only through a user behaviour override; opex fixed shares come from a two-point book fit when two comparable periods exist, else a pack classification stamped convention, and a pool nil on the book serves fixed share 0 by convention; a pool larger than revenue is served fixed; elasticity is 0 or 1; every served opex pool, unallocated_operating included, carries pool_level and pool_fixed_share keys expanded from one template entry each.

R15 WORKING-CAPITAL UNWIND [NEW]. A change in a receivables, inventory or payables target moves from the base target to the new target over the effective days in force (wc_unwind_ramp); the base run is unchanged to the cent.

R16 TAX [NEW]. Tax accrues on year-to-date pre-tax result within a plan year, may reverse within the year, never carries a loss across years (convention until a sourced rule is packed), and uses the book's effective rate where the engine's existing rule measures it (positive pre-tax and a reconstruction that reaches account 121), else the jurisdiction's packed statutory rate; when the book's rate is not measured, a jurisdiction with no packed rate, or a jurisdiction the anchor envelope does not record (0.3), refuses the plan rather than assume one.

R17 DEPRECIATION RATE [NEW]. The engine's closing-base rate stays the authority (it reproduces the anchor's own charge in plan year 1); history never changes the denominator; the pack's different quantity is renamed.

R18 DCF [NEW]. The cockpit DCF discounts the plan's own unlevered FCF at a WACC built from the book's cost of debt plus a user-stated equity premium, weights from the opening balance sheet, terminal growth from the inflation anchor or the user; every missing or non-meaningful input refuses by name; WACC is never adjusted; the valuation route's literal inputs are never used.

R19 SAVED CASES [NEW]. forecast_cases stores the canonical request and pins, never amounts; workspace members read, only the author writes; replay is byte-identical while pins hold and re-resolves with pins_changed otherwise; lever_set_hash is not a pin and case_id is not part of the canonical request; a table that is not visible answers 503 schema_not_migrated through a probe that never raises SystemExit.

R20 AI [NEW]. The model only proposes a default tier per driver among the served alternatives, with numeral-free text, through a route outside engine.forecast*; nothing changes without accept; no plan narrative, believe list or template rationale is built by this contract.

R21 SIGN FLIPS [NEW]. One classifier per runtime with seven kinds (compared, flip_to_negative, flip_to_positive, from_zero, to_zero, absent_base, absent_plan), orthogonal to the comparatives disclosure statuses; delta_pct only for compared with a non-zero base.

R22 DATA [NEW]. No client book is committed; a re-dated two-period pair is labelled SYNTHETIC wherever it is used; Scandia runs locally and prints aggregates only; the battery latency gate runs on the largest committed book.

R23 COPY [NEW]. Lever copy and templates are pack data with en and ro, served per book family; a family is used only when the structural signal is decided and does not disagree with the workspace; generic copy may contain no family's words; page chrome contains no industry word.

R24 PROFILES AND PROBES [NEW]. A lever release sends the surface's chart profile; tornado, break-evens and compares follow after a debounce; every probe projects in process and is never serialised; a refused probe is a refused row, a probe BalanceViolation is a 422.
